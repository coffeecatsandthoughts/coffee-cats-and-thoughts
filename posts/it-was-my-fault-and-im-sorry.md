A little story from my daughter's first week in junior high.

**Me:** How was school?

**K:** It was okay. Our classroom is sooo noisy. I got transferred seats twice because the teacher had to put me between two noisy classmates.

**Me:** Ohhh okay...

**K:** One kid was so noisy, the teacher threw chalk at him.

**Me:** *(I know this is gonna be an unpopular reaction with some parents, but yes! I giggled. No, actually, I laughed!)* Did he learn his lesson?

**K:** I don't think so. He was still noisy during the next subject.

**Me:** If you were the noisy one and your teacher did that to you... would you come running home to Mommy crying and offended?

**K:** No. It was my fault the teacher did it. I'd say sorry to the teacher, though.

**Me:** Cool.

Proud momma moment. 🥹🥹🥹

We can't control how others discipline, but we can raise kids who know how to take accountability. 🙏

Don't raise kids to be fragile. Raise them to be grounded, reflective, and strong enough to say:

**"It was my fault, and I'm sorry."**
