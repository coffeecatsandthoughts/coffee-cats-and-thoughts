<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Made sinigang this morning because apparently when life gets unhinged, I become a soup person now.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I don't even like sinigang that much. But here we are...</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Got a whole day of hospital things ahead; tests, discussions, people saying medical words at me with Very Serious Faces. And my brain said <em>you know what you should do? Cook. For an hour. In this Philippine heat.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So, I did. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And honestly? It slapped. Which is annoying because I wanted it to just be fine, not good, because now I have to admit that stress-cooking works and I am not ready to become that person.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My kids will come home later to a pot of sinigang I made before 9am on a doctor's appointment day, and they will not question it, because they know me. They know that sometimes Mom just<em> does things.</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I don't have a profound reason. The tamarind didn't heal me. The fish sauce didn't bring clarity. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>But I made something and it tasted good. And now I have to go let the doctor poke at me. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Could be worse.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The<em> Sinigang </em>was good. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
