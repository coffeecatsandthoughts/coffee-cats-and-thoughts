<p></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My son joined a few groups related to his interests, history, historical reenactments, and things that most teenagers his age probably don't spend their weekends doing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Last weekend, they had an event, so naturally Jay and I went with him.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I didn't think much about it. He's 16, but he's still my kid. Besides, if he's willing to spend an entire day talking about history, uniforms, and events from over a hundred years ago, the least I can do is show up.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When we got there, I realized most of the participants were young boys. For some reason, I expected to see other parents hanging around too. I didn't.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At one point, while I was taking their photos, one of them casually said, "Buti pa si Koko, supportive ang parents."</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The comment caught me off guard. Not because I thought we were doing anything special. I thought we were just doing what parents do. Showing up, taking photos, asking if they've eaten, and pretending not to embarrass them in front of their friends. 😅</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>We don't have a lot of extra time. Work, family, cats, deadlines, PTA, and life keep us pretty occupied. But for the things that matter to our kids, we try to make time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I hope our kids know that whatever path they choose in life, as long as it's good, honest, and doesn't involve me posting bail, their dad and I will always be in their corner.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Because sometimes support isn't about solving problems or giving advice. Sometimes it's just showing up.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>And maybe years from now, our children won't remember every word we said. But they'll remember we were there.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
