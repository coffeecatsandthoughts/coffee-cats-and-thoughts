<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Back in February, I posted this. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":583,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img src="https://coffeecatsandthoughts.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/thread.jpg?w=616" alt="" class="wp-image-583" /></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I honestly thought that was the end of the story. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This evening, while we were talking about tomorrow's seating arrangement for recognition day, my daughter mentioned that each student only gets one parent to sit beside them. Jay and I were trying to decide who would sit where when she casually said,  </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>Celine and City messaged me.. They said they want you to sit with us because they miss you...</em></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That caught me completely off guard. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Not because I think I'm anything special, but because if you had told me a year ago that teenagers would miss me before my PTA term had even ended, I would've laughed. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I walked into that school as a quiet, introverted, work-from-home mom who preferred staying in the background. I avoided small talk whenever I could. I certainly never imagined doing all these non-introverted PTA stuff.  Even more so getting to know so many students outside my own children's circles. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yet somehow, over the past year, I've gone from being "Kiera's mom" to someone a few other kids apparently look forward to seeing. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As my PTA term comes to an end, it's been teachers, staff, security guards, fellow parents, and now even the students saying they hope I continue.  </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Life has a funny way of changing us. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sometimes we say yes to something because of our own children. Then somewhere along the way, without even realizing it, we become part of other kids' school memories- that mom who always smiled, the parent who talked to them, the one who made them feel seen.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I don't know yet what happens after my term ends. But if this year taught me anything, it's that simply showing up matters more than we realize. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Apparently, people notice. Even the quiet kids. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
